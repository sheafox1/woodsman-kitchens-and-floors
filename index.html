How It Works

From showroom visit to finished kitchen

How a Woodsman project typically runs.

1. Visit the showroom

See cabinetry, countertops and flooring in person, or bring your own measurements.

2. Design consultation

Work one-on-one with a designer. Consultations are free; detailed drawings carry a design fee.

3. Choose your materials

Pick cabinet lines, quartz or granite, flooring and finishes with our team.

4. Installation

Our installers handle cabinets, countertops and flooring with precision.
